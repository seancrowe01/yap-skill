# Audience map

Fill this in before your first rep. The skill reads it at Stage 1 and grades topics against it.

## Centre

One exact viewer. Who they are, what they want, and the struggle in their own words. Quote a
real person if you can (a call, a DM, a comment). One paragraph.

Example shape: "[profession] who want [outcome] and cannot [the thing that is failing]."

## Rings

Each ring relaxes one constraint. Aim at the centre and ring 1. Rings 3 and 4 are where the
algorithm takes you later, not where you aim.

## White space

Topics that overperform when they appear but that nobody in your ring is making. Thin volume
plus high outlier multiple is the signature. List three to five with the evidence.

## Receipts you actually hold

Proof you can show on screen. Screenshots, numbers, consented client results. Each row must be
a file you can open, not a memory.

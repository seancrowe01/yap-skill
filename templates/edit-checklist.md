# Edit checklist

Your own edit order. The skill follows this at Stage 6. Edit the defaults to match how you
actually work, then keep it stable so every video looks like the same account.

## Order

1. Trim to zero dead air. No warm-up pause, no full second between sentences.
2. On-screen text as the outline. Headings, key points, lists. Two fonts maximum. Off the edges.
3. Zooms only where a line earns emphasis.
4. Overlays, screenshots and graphics tied to the exact words being said.
5. Captions, one line, near the face. Styled second to last.
6. Sound effects last. Five placements or fewer.

## Sound rules

- Effects 5 to 6 dB under the voice.
- Music near silent or off.
- Nothing lands on the last word of a line.

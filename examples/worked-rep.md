# Worked rep (illustrative)

Everything below is invented to show the shape of a rep. The account, the numbers and the
source video do not exist. Do not copy the numbers; copy the sequence.

## Stage 1: Topic

Watchlist pull, 90 days, sorted by multiple. Top candidates:

| # | Video | Multiple | Views | Fit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Never take a client call until you do this" | 41x | 210K | Ring 1 |
| 2 | "Why I stopped posting every day" | 18x | 95K | Centre |
| 3 | Generic discipline montage | 120x | 1.4M | Off-lane, said plainly |

User picks 2. Take test: the topic is obvious (everyone has an opinion on posting frequency).
What the user knows to be true that most do not: their last three clients booked from a
Thursday post each week, never from the daily filler. Example: a screenshot of the three
booking confirmations with dates. Implication: the calendar, not the volume, is the lever.

Gate question, user's words: "Yes. I think the day matters more than the count and I can show
it." Receipt: the booking screenshot, consent from all three clients on file. Saturation:
"post less" is a common take; the day-of-week angle is not. Proceed.

## Stage 2: Scaffold

Source: candidate 2, analysed, 18x. Sections kept: cold open (0 to 3 s, 7 percent), the
confession (3 to 14 s, 26 percent), the receipt (14 to 22 s, 19 percent), the rule (22 to 38 s,
38 percent), the door (38 to 42 s, 10 percent). Sections switched: none. Receipts: the booking
screenshot at `yap/receipts/bookings-q2.png`, dates verified against the calendar export.
No lines. Stop.

## Stage 3: Write

User's first line: "Most coaches are posting every day and it is doing nothing."

Grade: subject present (coaches), stakes weak ("doing nothing" is not a cost), plain words yes,
one meaning yes. Distance: one step. Verdict: passes three of four, stakes need a number the
user can prove. User has one from the receipt.

Continue line by line until the user has every section.

## Stage 4: Gate report

All fourteen gates listed with pass or fail and the reason. Two fails on the first pass: text
hook did not match spoken hook word for word, and the destination was two options instead of
one. User fixes both. Filming unblocked.

## Stage 5 to 7

Film card issued. Edit build named seven elements, sound last, effects under the voice. Rep
logged with hypothesis: "day-of-week receipt beats the volume argument". Sunday numbers fill
in the rest.

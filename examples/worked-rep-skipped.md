# Worked rep, the one that got skipped (illustrative)

Invented creator, invented numbers, invented source video. The point of this example is the
ending. Most reps should end here. If every topic you pick gets through, the gate is not
working.

The creator is an online yoga teacher with 3,200 followers who runs a 12 seat, 6 week course
and cannot fill it from Instagram. Her audience map centre reads: online yoga teachers under
5K who want a full cohort and cannot get Instagram to fill one. Her one receipt is a
screenshot of her March cohort, 11 of 12 seats filled, 9 of them from a story series.

## Stage 1: Topic

No research tool. She pastes three videos from accounts she follows, with her own note on
each channel's normal.

| # | Video | Views | Channel normal | Multiple | Fit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Stop teaching yoga to yoga teachers" | 41,000 | 3,000 | 13.7x | Centre. This is her white space sentence, said out loud by a wellness business coach |
| 2 | "My 5am routine" | 9,000 | 8,000 | 1.1x | Not an outlier. On her off-lane list too. Out |
| 3 | "How I filled my retreat with one story series" | 22,000 | 2,500 | 8.8x | Ring 1. Retreat not cohort, same mechanism |

She picks 1. Of course she does. It is the video she wishes she had made.

## The take test

**Is the topic non-obvious?** For her viewer, yes. Nobody in the centre is saying it. That
is exactly why the source did 13.7x. It is also why the bar for her version is higher, not
lower: the source already said the non-obvious thing. A second video saying the same
non-obvious thing is obvious.

**What does she know to be TRUE that most yoga teachers do not?**

Her answer: "That the people liking your posts are other teachers, not students, so the
likes mean nothing."

That is the source video's argument, restated. It is a good argument. It is not hers. Asked
what she has seen with her own eyes that proves it, she has a feeling and one DM from a
student who said the same thing. She has never checked who actually likes her posts.

**What EXAMPLE has she seen that most have not?**

None on this topic. Her receipt is about stories filling a cohort. It says nothing about
who likes her reels. The skill points this out and does not let the cohort screenshot be
borrowed as proof for a claim it does not prove.

**What IMPLICATION is nobody saying?**

She offers: "So you should stop posting reels." The skill flags that as the source's
implication again, and as a claim she would be making against her own offer, since her
course page still sends people to her reels.

**100 viewer test.** Not reached. There is no first line to test because there is no take.

## The gate question

Asked in her words: do you have a take on this that is different, that you are excited about?

Her answer, honestly: "I'm excited about it because it's true. I don't have a different
take. I'd be saying what she said."

## Verdict: skip

Three reasons, said plainly:

1. No take of her own. Her version would be a smaller account repeating a bigger account's
   argument. The viewer who has seen the original has no reason to watch hers.
2. No receipt for the claim. She cannot show who likes her reels. On a 3,200 follower account
   the proof has to be in the video, and she does not have it.
3. It fights her offer. Her course page routes through her reels. A video telling teachers
   to stop making reels is a leak from her own lane.

Nothing in the skill wrote a line, offered a take, or suggested an angle that would rescue
the topic. That is deliberate. A rescued topic is Claude's video, not hers.

## What she does instead, the same session

Two moves, both in the log.

**Bank the topic.** It goes in the skipped table of `yap/rep-log.md` with the reason and the
one thing that would unlock it: a week of checking who likes her posts and screenshotting
the profiles. If it turns out to be 80 percent teachers, she has a receipt nobody else has,
and the take becomes hers because she measured it. Then it comes back as a rep.

**Next candidate.** Video 3, 8.8x, clears 5x, can donate structure. Her receipt is about
exactly this: a story series filled 9 of 11 seats. She has a take she can prove and a number
she can put on screen. Stage 1 runs again from the take test, and this time it gets through.

The skip cost about four minutes. The alternative was a filmed, edited and posted video that
repeated someone else's point with no proof, on a small account, against her own offer.

## What to copy from this

- Pick the topic you are most excited about first. Then let the gate kill it. Excitement is
  not a take.
- When the take you give is the source's take, say so out loud. The skill will anyway.
- A receipt proves one thing. Do not stretch it over a second claim.
- Log the skip with the unlock condition. Half of the skipped topics come back once you go
  and get the receipt, and those are usually the best videos you make.
